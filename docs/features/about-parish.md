# About the Parish

## Purpose
Provide a trustworthy, parish-approved source of information about Notre-Dame-du-Secours.

## Sections
- Parish name and approved history.
- Church photographs and approved media.
- Official parish address and contact details.
- Tap-to-call and email actions only for confirmed public contact details.
- Map and directions using a confirmed map pin/address.
- Parish office or visiting information only when supplied and approved by the parish.
- App version and links to privacy information.

## Content approval
- Do not invent or infer the church's history, address, telephone number, email, priest's name, office hours, or map coordinates.
- All public parish information and media must be approved by the priest.
- Only the priest's specifically authorized administrator account may update shared About content.
- Ordinary users can view and use the contact/directions actions but cannot edit the content.

## Languages and accessibility
- Support Arabic (RTL), French (LTR), and Italian (LTR).
- Each language may have its own approved description. Clearly indicate when a translation is not yet available.
- Use accessible text sizes, descriptive image text, and large tap targets.

## Data and privacy
- Parish contact details are public only after parish approval.
- Do not display private personal contact details without explicit approval.
- External map apps may receive the selected public destination when the user chooses directions.
