# Parish Life & Activities

## Purpose
A read-only directory and activity feed that helps parishioners discover the life of the parish.

## Content
- Parish groups and ministries (for example, youth, children, choir, charity, and other groups only when confirmed by the parish).
- Group descriptions, meeting details, age guidance where appropriate, and approved contact route.
- Upcoming and past gatherings, retreats, community events, and activity summaries.
- Optional photo galleries using parish-approved images.
- Optional event registration may be considered later; it is not part of the initial release.

## Priest administrator
Only the priest's specifically authorized administrator account may create, edit, publish, archive, or remove shared group and activity content, upload approved photos, and update event details.

## Ordinary users
Users may browse groups, activities, event details, and approved galleries; save items to personal favorites; and use device sharing where appropriate. They cannot modify shared parish content.

## Languages and privacy
- Arabic (RTL), French (LTR), and Italian (LTR).
- Provide translated content where approved; do not invent group names, schedules, contacts, or event details.
- Obtain appropriate permission before publishing identifiable photos, especially photos of children.
- Do not expose private member contact information. Prefer parish-approved contact channels.

## Security
Enforce all shared-content write permissions in backend/database rules. Hiding edit controls in the app is not sufficient. Keep the priest administrator identity securely configured and maintain an audit trail where supported.
