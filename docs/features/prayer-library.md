# Prayer Library — Arabic, French & Italian

The Prayer Library supports Arabic (RTL), French (LTR), and Italian (LTR). Current starter content includes the Our Father, Hail Mary, Glory Be, a thanksgiving prayer, and a prayer for peace.

## Available reader features

- Browse prayers by category.
- Search prayer titles and text in the available languages.
- Open a prayer in a focused reading view.
- Increase or decrease the prayer text size while reading.
- Copy a prayer or use the device's native share sheet when supported.
- Save and remove prayers from personal favorites (stored locally on the device).

Audio recordings are not yet available. They will be added only after suitable language/accent voices are verified and the final wording is reviewed and approved by parish clergy.

## Content and permissions

Starter translations are for review and must not be presented as clergy-approved. Shared prayer content remains priest-controlled. Ordinary users can browse and save favorites locally; they cannot edit shared prayer texts.

## Future work

- Approved recordings and offline audio playback.
- Offline reading cache.
- Expand the library with morning/evening prayers, prayers for the sick, Communion, and special occasions.
