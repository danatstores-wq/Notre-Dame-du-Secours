# Prayer Library — Arabic, French & Italian

The Prayer Library supports Arabic (RTL), French (LTR), and Italian (LTR). Current content includes the parish-supplied Arabic Nicene Creed, Our Father, Hail Mary, and Salve Regina. French and Italian texts for the standard common prayers have now been aligned with official Catholic sources: the Vatican Compendium of the Catechism and, for the current national liturgical wording of the Our Father, the French Bishops’ Conference and the Italian Bishops’ Conference (CEI). Arabic text for the Glory Be, thanksgiving prayer, and prayer for peace is marked as awaiting the parish's approved wording.

## Available reader features

- Browse prayers by category.
- Search prayer titles and text in the available languages.
- Open a prayer in a focused reading view.
- Increase or decrease the prayer text size while reading.
- Copy a prayer or use the device's native share sheet when supported.
- Save and remove prayers from personal favorites (stored locally on the device).

Audio recordings are not yet available. They will be added only after suitable language/accent voices are verified and the final wording is reviewed and approved by parish clergy.

## Content and permissions

Arabic wording supplied for the Nicene Creed, Our Father, Hail Mary, and Salve Regina has been inserted verbatim and still requires final clergy review before publication. Other Arabic prayer texts are not displayed as completed prayers until their approved wording is supplied. Shared prayer content remains priest-controlled. Ordinary users can browse and save favorites locally; they cannot edit shared prayer texts.

## Official French and Italian text sources

The following official Catholic sources were used for the standard prayers:

- Vatican, *Compendium of the Catechism of the Catholic Church* (French): https://www.vatican.va/archive/compendium_ccc/documents/archive_2005_compendium-ccc_fr.html
- Vatican, *Compendio del Catechismo della Chiesa Cattolica* (Italian): https://www.vatican.va/archive/compendium_ccc/documents/archive_2005_compendium-ccc_it.html
- French Bishops’ Conference liturgy resource explaining the currently approved French wording of the Our Father, including “Et ne nous laisse pas entrer en tentation”: https://liturgie.catholique.fr/la-messe/la-liturgie-eucharistique/309766-ne-nous-laisse-pas-entrer-en-tentation-note-exegetique/
- Italian Bishops’ Conference (CEI), official liturgical resource showing the current wording “non abbandonarci alla tentazione”: https://liturgico.chiesacattolica.it/wp-content/uploads/sites/8/2025/12/03/vespri-17-23dicembre-def-2.pdf

The French and Italian standard texts now covered by these sources are the Our Father, Hail Mary, Glory Be, Nicene-Constantinopolitan Creed, and Salve Regina. The French and Italian thanksgiving and peace texts are parish-composed draft prayers; they are explicitly titled as parish prayers and are not represented as universal official prayers. They still require clergy review.
