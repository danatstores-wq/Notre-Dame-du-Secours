Arabic Bible text: Smith–Van Dyck Arabic Bible (1865), public-domain translation.
Source dataset: Midvash bible-data, versions/ar/svd: https://github.com/midvash/bible-data/tree/main/versions/ar/svd
Source metadata identifies this translation as public domain and records 66 books, 1,189 chapters, and 31,104 verses.
This edition contains 66 books (Protestant canon). It does NOT include the seven Catholic Deuterocanonical books, nor the Catholic additions to Esther and Daniel. It must not be described as a complete 73-book Catholic Bible.
